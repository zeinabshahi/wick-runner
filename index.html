FAQ

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. Open the page and play. No sign-up, no wallet, no payment.

Does auto-run earn the score for me?

Auto-run keeps you moving. Distance gives only 2 points per metre, and pickups give 10 flat points. Intentional jumps, slides, dashes and grabs build your skill chain; unprompted automatic crossings never create a combo or Flow. Your result shows the full score breakdown.

Are the charts real market data?

No. Every chart is generated for gameplay and says nothing about the price of any token.

Do points or tiers have value?

No. XP, Season Points, tiers and trails are in-game progress only. They are not tokens and have no monetary value.

Do I need a wallet?

No. Practice needs nothing. For ranked play and on-chain records you sign in with your email: Privy creates a secure wallet for you on Robinhood Chain testnet, so there is nothing to install and no browser wallet is used. Recording a run on-chain is a testnet transaction; the game never requests a token approval.

Is this an official vibe/vibe game?

No. Wick Runner is an independent project launching on vibe/vibe. It is not operated, endorsed or audited by vibe/vibe.
