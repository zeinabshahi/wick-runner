Wick Runner turns a candlestick chart into a parkour course. Green candles hold, red ones crack under you. Chain jumps, ride the wicks, dodge laser gates and mines, and race the ghost of your best run.

$WRUN is the Wick Runner community token. It unlocks cosmetic perks and verified arenas, never leaderboard points: the boards stay pure skill.
